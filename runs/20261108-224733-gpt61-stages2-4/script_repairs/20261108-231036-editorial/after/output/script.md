## 3. Questions and tracking

AI answers are probabilistic: repeating the same question can produce different brands. Searchbloom's report calls for daily tracking of fixed, real buyer questions across the AI platforms buyers use, then reading the trend. According to Searchbloom, branded and non-branded prompts should be separated and segmented by awareness, consideration, and purchase. For measurement details, Searchbloom says to ask which tracking tool, prompts, platforms, and run frequency an agency uses.
